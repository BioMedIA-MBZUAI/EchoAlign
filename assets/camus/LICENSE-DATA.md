# Data license

The videos and ring positions in this folder are derived from the CAMUS dataset.

CAMUS is released under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0
license (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode

The CAMUS terms also say:

1. The dataset may be used free of charge for non-commercial scientific research only.
2. Any use must cite:

   S. Leclerc, E. Smistad, J. Pedrosa, A. Ostvik, et al. "Deep Learning for Segmentation
   using an Open Large-Scale Dataset in 2D Echocardiography", IEEE Transactions on Medical
   Imaging, vol. 38, no. 9, pp. 2198-2210, Sept. 2019. doi: 10.1109/TMI.2019.2900516

The derived videos and positions in this folder are shared under the same license.
